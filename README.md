## How I approach IT work
- **Start with the operational need** — understand what staff need to get done, and the constraints of a healthcare setting.
- **Think across systems** — identity, devices, applications, security, and the user's workflow together.
- **Automate repeatable work** — build integrations that cut down recurring manual administration.
- **Validate the outcome** — test installation, service operation, and access separately, keeping recovery options open.
- **Document the evidence** — record what worked, what failed, and what still needs validation.

I use AI-assisted research and scripting as part of my workflow, alongside hands-on administration and troubleshooting.
